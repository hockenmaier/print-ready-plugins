# Print Ready

Describe a part, supply its measurements and refine it through conversation. Print Ready gives your AI assistant tools to create OpenSCAD models, inspect actual geometry previews and deliver STL files for slicing. Make replacement parts, brackets, organizers, holders, adapters, enclosures and personalized objects.

## Get started

Open this plugin's Connectors tab and connect Print Ready. The connector uses https://api.printreadyparts.com/mcp. Describe your part and its dimensions. Ask for the welcome guide when you want help or examples. For example: Make a 40 by 20 by 6 mm PLA plate with a centered 6 mm through-hole. Show the finished part and its STL download.

Create, render and download parts as a guest. Sign in only when you want a private saved library or printing preferences. Ask to sign in, then ask to save your current parts. A compatible host can display a rotatable 3D viewer; other clients can use actual PNG previews and download links. Verify physical fit and printing settings before printing.

Print Ready uses the same hosted service and shared modeling instructions across supported AI tools, including ChatGPT and Claude. The plugin connects to that service; it does not run an OpenSCAD compiler on your computer. Editable source is available on request.
